# Limitations and rejected simplifications

- A scalar option-growth or depletion coefficient does not predict the retained
  intervention effects.
- A lower discounted option value need not imply lower gain at every finite
  budget.
- Static breadth, total strength, or structural coverage alone does not explain
  long correction trajectories.
- Generic preservation can hinder feature creation and recovery.
- Identical extra parameters do not reproduce natural widening.
- Width does not automatically slow features under the toy's mean-field metric.
- ReLU boundary crossings can change conclusions under step refinement.
- Cumulative learning identities account for observed loss but do not by
  themselves establish causal competition or generalization.
- MNIST effects are mostly local and transient; natural width mediation remains
  open.

results, is available at commit `92b7904`.
