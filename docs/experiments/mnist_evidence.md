# MNIST evidence

MNIST is a paused transfer setting, not the active source of theory changes.

The maintained local model predicts controlled finite-budget intervention
contrasts from known checkpoints. On ten fresh seeds, one midpoint gate refresh
reduced error from .237 to .091 of a zero-effect baseline with 118/120 correct
signs. An eight-seed focused replication achieved .117 with 96/96 signs.

The causal effects are modest. Peer-only pressure causes some immediate
correction impairments, and matched preservation partially rescues selected
cases, but impairments generally disappear within five correction steps.
Preservation often reduces response drift while worsening longer correction and
ordinary full-task recovery. A digit-specific persistent effect did not
replicate.

Consequently, the evidence supports a local response-evolution predictor on real
images. It does not establish widespread persistent competition, predict whole
MNIST runs, or mediate the ordinary width advantage.
