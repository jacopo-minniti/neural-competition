# Theory and prospective records

[Minimal model and probabilistic option transitions](minimal_probabilistic_options.md)
keeps spectral capability and residual demand as the core state, demotes the
exact implementation distribution to supporting theory, and validates frozen
closure/reopening probabilities on fresh natural widths and frequency families.
See [raw forecasts, calibration limits and reproduction](probabilistic_options/README.md).

[Functional implementation state and refinement hierarchies](functional_implementation_state.md)
adds a feature-shape resource measure, exact gate-conditioned moment dynamics,
and causal counterexamples to global moment closure. Natural hierarchy experiments
separate structural recovery from timely rare correction; [evidence](implementation_state/README.md)
retains failed interventions and a plateau removed by step refinement.

[Heterogeneous demand, shared implementations, and width](heterogeneous_option_resources.md)
is the preceding controlled-toy causal phase; MNIST is paused. It formalizes intrinsic
option interactions, probabilistic width effects, and tests pressure removal and
same-width directional recruitment. See [reproduction and raw evidence](toy_resource_competition/README.md).

[Finite-budget competition and width](finite_budget_competition.md) adds a
budget-matched causal criterion, one midpoint mask refresh, and conditional
width-protection bounds. Fresh MNIST tests predict both local impairments and
their frequent disappearance; targeted replication rejects a digit-specific
persistent-obstruction story. See the [audited evidence](causal_response/README.md).

[A local model of learning-driven response changes](local_response_model.md)
keeps coefficient–feature coupling and physical response evaluation while
testing a fixed-mask approximation to parameter evolution. Locked MNIST
interventions distinguish predictable local response changes from ordinary loss
interference; scalar closures, long-horizon errors and weak causal effects are
retained in the [evidence](response_feedback/README.md).

[Observed learning, sampling, and finite updates](observed_learning_and_sampling.md)
adds an exact finite-SGD ledger, tests small-panel response bias and batch-size
effects on MNIST, and states a margin condition for the toy small-step account.
Its [evidence directory](observed_learning/README.md) includes fresh pairs,
historical replay, negative measurement results, and numerical boundary checks.
The [current research context](../research_context.md) links the maintained framework.

[Delayed demand and option recovery](delayed_demand_option_recovery.md) extends
the functional GD-response framework with task-conditioned transport,
response-matched implementation interventions, and finite-budget recovery bounds.
It tests shared rules with later-important contextual refinements, preserving
both the positive discrete sequence and continuous recovery counterexamples.
The [evidence directory](option_transport_resilience/README.md) contains raw
records, prospective forecasts, source snapshots, and reproduction commands.

[Competition definition and forecasts](competition_definition.md) gives a precise
coupling counterfactual, residual-weighted spectral damage and its general-metric
extension. [Protocol](coupled_modes_protocol.md) records the MNIST forecasting tests.

[Signed capacity](signed_capacity.md) derives a Gaussian second-Hermite error
bound for missing positive/negative roles. The [sign-survival protocol](sign_survival_protocol.md)
records the targeted intervention and the chronology of its follow-ups.

The [profiled-feature report](../reports/feature_profile.md) derives the current
feature/readout decomposition and moving-optimum tracking equation, with their
limitations. Its [protocol](feature_profile_protocol.md) records the main design
and explicitly labels controls added after observing results.

[Signed residual potentials](residual_growth.md) is the maintained theory note.
It derives the signed-amplitude growth law, explains what assumptions make
correct-polarity suppression possible, and separates that local statement from
the failed global sign predictions. Its teacher-free extension is a proposal,
not a completed MNIST result.

Two unchanged documents preserve what was predicted before the corresponding
experiments; read their outcomes in the [report](../reports/residual_growth.md):

- [Conditional-growth protocol](residual_growth_protocol.md): its sign did not
  predict where ordinary training showed the largest width benefit.
- [Polarity follow-up protocol](polarity_followup_protocol.md): the refined sign
  and bias controls also did not establish a global width-benefit boundary.

Older landscape, kernel, allocation and splitting notes are consolidated in
[research history](../reports/research_history.md), with complete originals in
Git commit `b03c53f`. Related literature is cited where used in the maintained
note. No theorem from a different model or optimizer is treated as a guarantee
for the present experiments.
